# Sophia's Imagination revenue model

**Status:** planning only — no checkout, payment processor, subscription,
affiliate program, advertising unit, paid collaboration, or revenue is verified
as live in the reviewed records.

**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-24 AKDT

Sophia's Imagination is an audience brand and a public demonstration of an
honestly synthetic persona. This document translates the supplied six-year
portfolio brief into an evidence-gated operating model. It is not a forecast,
a valuation, a promise of income, or authorization to publish, sell, spend,
contact a partner, change an account, or enable a provider.

## Present commercial truth

- The site is a static editorial experience with no analytics, cookies,
  accounts, forms, or commerce.
- Two public song pages have completed the site's current general-audience
  evidence review. They are not offered for sale here, and their presence does
  not prove commercial-distribution rights.
- The ten gallery images in this repository are 800 × 1067 web derivatives.
  They are not represented as print masters or sale-ready inventory.
- External profile cards remain withheld pending account-name, disclosure,
  ownership, and public-story reconciliation.
- The private 30-item campaign bundle is awaiting owner approval. A staged
  bundle is not a published campaign, an audience result, or a sales channel.

## First-dollar sequence

1. **Finish identity, rights, and disclosure decisions.** Resolve the public
   account set, keep the general-audience story boundary explicit, and complete
   the private source/right records for any asset proposed for sale or license.
2. **Complete one evidenced participation loop.** Use the existing protocol to
   prove that a public choice can be opened, closed, counted, corrected, and
   archived without invented engagement. A later, separately approved print
   choice may inform candidate selection; C01 is not automatically a sales poll.
3. **Prove one print before opening a shop.** Select one source master, clear its
   commercial-use record, produce one physical proof, inspect it, and record
   true unit economics. The current web derivatives must not be sent to a
   print-on-demand provider as production masters.
4. **Run a deliberately small open-edition print pilot.** Only after seller identity, tax,
   payment, fulfillment, customer support, shipping, privacy, returns, and
   accessibility are approved and test-verified. Start with one or two
   products; do not manufacture scarcity or imply demand.
5. **Treat music monetization as a separate gate.** A paid release, delivery to
   streaming services, sync pitch, or license requires the owner-approved
   master, source lineage, contributor permissions, voice/source
   authorization, applicable tool terms, and destination-specific disclosure.
6. **Consider collaborations and virtual-fashion licensing only from evidence.**
   Use a written scope, approved assets, synthetic-character disclosure,
   editorial independence, usage limits, term, territory, compensation,
   approval rights, and takedown/correction terms. Never imply real product use
   or a physical shoot.
7. **Use a paid MyPersonas page only after that platform's independent gates
   pass.** This site does not authorize billing, republication, or provider
   activation elsewhere.

The exact activation controls for the first print proof and pilot are in
`docs/COMMERCE-READINESS.md`.

## Measurement convention to approve

- The portfolio brief defines Year 1 as 2026-10-01 through 2027-09-30. Preserve
  that fiscal window if the ranges are accepted; do not silently substitute a
  calendar year or launch-to-date total.
- Any accepted revenue target must state whether it means gross receipts, net
  revenue, or contribution. Before activation, the owner/accounting reviewer
  must decide whether it excludes collected tax and customer-paid shipping. In every
  case, refunds, provider/processor fees, production cost, shipping subsidy,
  support time, and net contribution must be reported separately.
- `Audience` is not presently measurable on-site because the site deliberately
  has no analytics, accounts, or email capture. Provider-native followers,
  reach, video views, song plays, unique site visitors, subscribers, and buyers
  are different metrics and must never be merged into one audience claim.
- If the owner wants an on-site audience target, first approve a
  privacy-respecting measurement method and update the privacy record before
  collection. Until then, internal audience targets remain unverified planning
  inputs and must not become public claims.

## Evidence required before expansion

For each activated engine, record a dated baseline and measure only what the
approved provider can verify. At minimum:

- **Print pilot:** orders, gross receipts, provider fees, taxes, shipping,
  refunds/replacements, damaged orders, net contribution, support time, and
  proof defects.
- **Music:** destination, release status, gross and net receipts, rights splits,
  corrections/takedowns, and audience data with its source and date.
- **Collaboration or licensing:** signed scope, licensed assets, term,
  territory, deliverables, approval status, compensation, disclosure, and any
  correction or removal.
- **Audience:** source, date range, and definition for every count. Do not merge
  followers, views, streams, visits, subscribers, or buyers into one number.

Expand only after a completed pilot shows acceptable quality, support load,
rights compliance, and unit economics. If a proof fails or the operating gates
remain unresolved, keep the site editorial rather than opening a nominal shop.

## Pivot and stop rules

- If identity/disclosure decisions or private rights records remain unresolved,
  continue the editorial site and participation work; do not move the shop date
  to disguise the dependency.
- If the first proof fails, the owner may approve one specific revision or
  reject the product. A failed proof does not authorize an automatic alternate
  SKU, provider, spend, or upscale.
- Before a pilot opens, set written minimum contribution, maximum defect,
  refund/replacement, delivery, and support-load thresholds. Pause new orders
  if any threshold fails or cannot be measured.
- If a bounded pilot does not establish repeatable demand and acceptable
  operations during its owner-approved window, retain prints as an occasional
  editorial experiment rather than expanding catalog or infrastructure.
- If a collaboration asks the project to conceal synthetic origins, imply a
  physical endorsement, or surrender required approval/correction rights, do
  not proceed regardless of fee.

## Required owner decisions before activation

- Approve the official public account set and synthetic-character disclosures.
- Approve the exact source master and private rights record for the first print
  proof.
- Choose the legal seller, jurisdiction, payment route, fulfillment provider,
  customer-support channel, privacy terms, and returns/refund approach.
- Approve a proof budget before any order or paid provider action.
- Approve each commercial music use and its complete private evidence package.
- Approve any collaboration or license in writing before outreach or delivery.
