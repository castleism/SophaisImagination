# Commerce readiness — prints, music, and licensing

**Status:** preparation only. No product, checkout, payment route, fulfillment
provider, commercial music distribution, sync pitch, or brand collaboration is
approved or represented as live.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-24 AKDT

## Current inventory finding

The repository contains ten catalog concepts in JPEG and WebP form. Every one
is recorded in `assets/provenance.json` as an 800 × 1067 public web derivative,
not a source master. At 300 pixels per inch, that supports only about 2.7 × 3.6
inches before cropping. Those files are suitable for the present web gallery;
they are not evidence of a print-ready product.

No sellable print SKU is ready in this repository. A public image, a hash, a
gallery title, or an archived source record is not by itself proof of
commercial rights, adequate resolution, color quality, fulfillment readiness,
or customer-facing terms.

## Gate A — source and rights

Before a concept becomes a print candidate, the private record must contain:

- owner-approved source master filename, dimensions, color space, byte size,
  and SHA-256;
- creation and edit lineage, tool/model version, and applicable commercial-use
  terms for the creation date;
- contributor, uploaded-source, sample, likeness, trademark, and other
  third-party-rights findings, including an explicit `none identified` where
  appropriate;
- confirmation that the image is a clearly adult fictional character in an
  imagined setting and does not expose private story material;
- exact title, crop, edition language, disclosure, allowed products, and
  destination approved by the owner.

The public repository should receive only the approved public label and a hash
for the final customer-facing derivative. Private prompts, source locations,
billing evidence, rejected work, and credentials stay outside it.

## Gate B — physical proof

For one owner-approved candidate:

1. Export the required provider profile and dimensions from the approved source
   master; do not upscale the 800 × 1067 web file and call it a master.
2. Inspect crop, facial continuity, hands, fabric edges, typography if any,
   noise, banding, highlights, shadows, and disclosure placement at 100%.
3. Order exactly one proof only after provider, price, shipping, and budget are
   approved.
4. Record provider, product, size, substrate, print location, cost, shipping,
   order date, delivery date, and proof-file hash privately.
5. Photograph the received proof under neutral light and record color, crop,
   damage, packaging, and finish findings.
6. Mark the candidate `accepted`, `revise`, or `rejected`. A failed proof does
   not authorize an automatic reorder or a different product.

## Gate C — operator, policy, and money

Before any customer can pay, approve and test:

- legal seller identity and jurisdiction;
- tax collection, reporting, and payout responsibility;
- payment and fulfillment providers, current fees, account permissions, and
  data handling;
- truthful production time, shipping regions, customs/duties, and inventory or
  made-to-order language;
- returns, refunds, replacements, cancellations, chargebacks, lost/damaged
  orders, and customer-support ownership;
- privacy and cookie changes required by the actual checkout flow;
- accessible product copy, image alternatives, price, size, material, and
  synthetic-media disclosure;
- a sandbox or low-value test order, confirmation, fulfillment, tracking,
  cancellation/refund, payout, and reconciliation path.

No provider account, paid plan, tax setting, DNS route, checkout, or product
listing may be activated from this document alone.

## Minimum provenance label

Adapt this only after the exact work and product are approved:

> **[Work title]** — a disclosed AI-created visual concept of Sophia, a
> fictional adult character, developed under human direction and review. The
> setting and scene are imagined. Product size, material, fulfillment method,
> and edition status are stated separately and must match the delivered item.

Do not say `limited edition` without a fixed edition size and an enforced
inventory record. Do not say `archival`, `museum quality`, `sustainable`,
`handmade`, or equivalent without current product-specific evidence.

## Music and sync gate

The two reviewed public song pages may remain in the editorial record. Before a
paid release, sync pitch, sale, or license, the private package must include the
owner-authorized master and hash, lyric version, source and edit lineage,
voice/source authorization, stems or an explicit unavailable finding,
contributors and splits, commercial-use terms, artwork/video rights,
destination metadata, and the exact approved disclosure. Public-page presence
and an owner plan attestation do not replace the rest of that package.

## Collaboration and licensing gate

Before outreach or delivery, define in writing:

- the real counterparty and authorized contact;
- licensed asset, media, territory, term, channels, exclusivity, edits, and
  sublicensing;
- compensation, expenses, payment timing, cancellation, and usage reporting;
- owner review/approval, synthetic-character disclosure, and correction or
  takedown rights;
- a ban on claims that Sophia attended, wore, used, traveled to, performed at,
  or personally endorsed anything in the physical world.

## Stop conditions

Stop without opening sales when the source master is missing, rights are
unclear, the proof fails, the seller or tax owner is unresolved, provider terms
are unacceptable, support/returns are unowned, unit economics are negative or
unknown, disclosure is incomplete, or the owner has not approved the exact
product and spend.
