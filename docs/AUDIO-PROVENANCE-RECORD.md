## Required private record — one row per selected output

Keep the following outside this public repository:

- owner approval record and intended use;
- generation date and time, account, the owner plan-at-generation attestation, dated billing-period corroboration, and the terms version that applied;
- tool and model version, source/model or voice identifier, exact prompts, settings, seeds, and edit/extension lineage;
- source-story and lyric version hashes, authorship and contributor permissions;
- uploaded audio, samples, interpolations, remixes, extensions, and their licenses or a recorded `none` finding;
- selected master filename, format, duration, byte size, SHA-256, allowed download path, and any embedded provenance metadata;
- stems and their hashes, or an explicit statement that stems are unavailable;
- rejected/alternate output identifiers sufficient to reconstruct selection history without publishing them;
- artwork/video tool records, source stills, output hashes, and disclosure text;
- destination URL/ID, publication timestamp, platform disclosure, corrections, takedowns, and rights disputes.

Never stream-rip a public page to manufacture a “master” hash. Hash the owner-authorized downloaded master and preserve the allowed-download evidence with the private record.
