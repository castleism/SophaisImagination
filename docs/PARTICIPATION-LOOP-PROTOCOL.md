# First public participation loop — evidence protocol

Status: **proposed, owner approval required**. No vote has opened, no response has been counted, and no archive result exists.

## Phase 1 — bounded signature choice

The recommended first archiveable choice is the existing C01 prompt: **pearls, laurel, or light?** C01 is now the next identity/campaign post, not the account's first-ever post.

Publication remains blocked until the owner approves the exact destination, caption, image, alt text, disclosure, future time, and account health. Content approval does not authorize publishing.

If published, record these rules on or from the official source post:

- Source: one permanent official post URL; replies on copied posts or other platforms do not count.
- Window: open for 168 hours from the provider timestamp; record open and close in UTC.
- Method: one public reply naming `pearls`, `laurel`, or `light` per account.
- Eligibility: the first unambiguous eligible choice from an account counts. Obvious duplicates, bots, spam, deleted/unavailable replies, choices after close, and replies naming multiple options are invalid.
- Corrections: a voter may replace their choice with a later unambiguous reply before close; record only the final eligible choice and preserve the audit note.
- Tie: publish the tied counts and run one 48-hour runoff among the tied options. Do not break a tie privately.
- Zero valid replies: close with `0`, declare no winner, and do not imply participation occurred.
- Evidence: capture the provider post ID/URL, timestamp, visible replies at close, exclusions with reasons, total valid responses, and per-option counts in the private record.
- Credit: individual voters are not named. Quote or credit a reply only after explicit permission and approval of the exact public name or pseudonym.
- Correction policy: if a count changes, preserve the original record, append the corrected count/date/reason, and visibly correct any public result that used it.

Copy `docs/COLLABORATION-RECORD-TEMPLATE.md` only after the source post is live. Never prefill the source URL, timestamps, counts, winner, or result.

## Phase 2 — Three-Word Worlds submissions

C10 is a submission ritual, not automatically a vote. A valid submission contains exactly three original, evocative words and no protected character, brand, real-person likeness, living-artist imitation request, defamation, sexualized minor content, or claimed real event.

The owner-editor may shortlist usable submissions for visual and storytelling potential. If the project wants an audience-selected winner, publish the shortlisted combinations as a separate bounded choice with its own source URL, close rule, eligibility, tie rule, and counts. Do not describe an editorial selection as a popular vote.

Before using or publicly crediting a submitted combination, obtain permission for the words to be used in a disclosed synthetic concept and separately obtain the contributor's preferred public name, pseudonym, or no-credit choice. Keep the consent record private.

## Archive threshold

The collaboration archive changes only after a real choice closes and the owner approves an evidence-backed record. A post, prompt, view count, like, or open submission request is not a completed vote.
