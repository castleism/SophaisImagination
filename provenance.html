Transparency record

Media provenance, without theater

Every catalog image on this site is disclosed synthetic media shaped by human art direction and review. The public record anchors the exact web derivatives; it does not pretend a checksum can replace the private source and generation archive.

View the machine-readable record (JSON)

What the record proves

  • The deployed derivative's filename, format, dimensions, byte size, and SHA-256.
  • Whether a later copy is byte-for-byte identical to the listed public asset.
  • That the public catalog is labeled synthetic rather than documentary.

What it does not prove

  • A prompt, model, date, or source file that is not included in the record.
  • Ownership, consent, or commercial rights by checksum alone.
  • That web resizing or conversion retained source metadata or content credentials.

Credential boundary: source and generation records are held in the owner's private archive. Public JPEG, PNG, and WebP derivatives should not be assumed to retain a source file's metadata or content credentials. This page distinguishes that limit instead of overstating provenance.
